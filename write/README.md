# write

systems for producing and repairing writing. these are for writers, editors and founders who publish often and want a consistent voice without a committee.

- **[stop telling writers to match the voice. measure it.](voice-profile/README.md)**  
  every team says "match our voice" and almost none can say what it means. turn the writing you've published into a profile you can check a draft against. skill: `voice-profile`
