# about

i'm shashank. online i sometimes go by stupidpreneur, and people describe me as an idiot savant.

i'm the co-founder of [ellora](https://getellora.com), the ai content editor inside chatgpt, claude and the other apps your team writes in. it learns how your brand sounds and fixes each draft before anyone has to.

most of my work is turning a messy job into a process that runs the same way twice. that might be shipping code with a coding agent, building a voice profile from years of posts, measuring something that moves every day, or fixing the broken links in my own products. the job changes. the habit doesn't. i write down the steps, add a gate where things usually go wrong, and keep the evidence.

this site is where those processes go once they've held up in real work. every post is one workflow with the exact steps. every post also comes with a skill you can download and run in your own agent, so you don't have to rebuild it from the post.

if you run one and it breaks, or you get stuck on a step, [open an issue on github](https://github.com/shashank-sn/blog/issues/new/choose). that's the best way to reach me, and it helps the next person who hits the same thing.
